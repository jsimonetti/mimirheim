# Context Map

## Contexts

- [Config Service](./mimirheim_shared/CONTEXT.md): protocol and vocabulary for how mimirheim core and helpers expose, present, and accept edits to their own configuration
- [Solver and Devices](./mimirheim/CONTEXT.md): vocabulary for energy planning, device behavior, and the boundary between mimirheim's schedule and local device controls

Individual helper domains under `mimirheim_helpers/` have not been modeled yet and are left for a future session.
